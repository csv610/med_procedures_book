\chapter{Stem Cell Transplant}

\section*{Overview}
A stem cell transplant replaces damaged or destroyed bone marrow stem cells with healthy stem cells. Can be autologous (self) or allogeneic (donor).

\section*{Alternative Names}
Hematopoietic stem cell transplant, HSCT, Stem cell therapy

\section*{Principle}
Ablative conditioning destroys the recipient's diseased or deficient hematopoietic system; infused donor or autologous stem cells then migrate to the marrow, engraft, and reconstitute normal blood and immune cell production.
\section*{History}
E. Donnall Thomas performed the first successful human marrow transplants in the 1950s, winning the 1990 Nobel Prize.

\section*{Why It Is Done}
Ordered to treat hematological malignancies (like leukemia, lymphoma, myeloma) or severe bone marrow failure (aplastic anemia).

\section*{Is This a Primary or Secondary Test or Procedure}
Primary curative treatment for specific high-risk leukemias and lymphomas.

\section*{How It Is Performed}
Following high-dose conditioning chemotherapy or radiation, healthy stem cells are infused intravenously, where they travel to the bone marrow and engraft.

\section*{Preparation}
Conditioning regimen (chemo/radiation) to ablate native marrow; place central venous catheter; patient isolation.

\section*{Contraindications and Precautions}
Severe organ dysfunction (heart, liver, lungs), active uncontrolled infection, or poor performance status.

\section*{Risks}
Graft-versus-host disease (GVHD, in allogeneic), severe infections (due to neutropenia), mucositis, or hepatic sinusoidal obstruction syndrome.

\section*{Aftercare and Recovery}
Strict isolation in a positive-pressure room; administer prophylactic antimicrobials and G-CSF; monitor CBC daily for engraftment.

\section*{Outcome and Follow-up}
Success is indicated by engraftment (recovery of ANC >500 and platelets >20,000 for 3 consecutive days) and tumor remission.

\section*{Expected Results}
Successful engraftment; complete donor chimerism (for allogeneic); absence of active GVHD.

\section*{Follow-up}
Frequent outpatient clinic visits, bone marrow biopsy at day 100, and revaccination schedule.
